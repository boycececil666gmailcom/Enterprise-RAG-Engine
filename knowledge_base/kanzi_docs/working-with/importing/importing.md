# Importing

When you want to bring to your Kanzi Studio project assets you created with a third-party tool, you need to import or merge the assets. Kanzi Studio supports importing of:

- **3D assets** such as meshes, animations, and splines. See Preparing 3D assets in third-party tools, Importing 3D assets, Importing animations, and Merging 3D assets.
- **Images**. See Importing images and Importing Adobe Photoshop PSD files.
- **Fonts**. See Importing fonts.

You can bring content from other Kanzi Studio projects into your project. See Importing projects, Merging projects, and Combining Kanzi Studio projects into a Kanzi application.
